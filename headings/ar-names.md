# Arabic name forms — counted in the shipped Van Dyck text, not assumed

## The orthographic rule that governs everything

**Van Dyck writes word-initial hamza as a bare alif.** Every proper name that modern standard
Arabic spells with أ or إ appears here with ا, and the hamza-carrying form scores **zero**:

| English | Van Dyck | count | modern form | count |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jerusalem | اورشليم | 814 | أورشليم | **0** |
| Israel | اسرائيل | 2596 | إسرائيل | **0** |
| Abraham | ابراهيم | 248 | إبراهيم | **0** |
| Isaac | اسحق | 146 | إسحاق | **0** |
| Ishmael | اسماعيل | 47 | إسماعيل | **0** |
| Elijah | ايليا | 102 | إيليا | **0** |
| Elisha | اليشع | 59 | إليشع | **0** |
| Isaiah | اشعياء | 53 | إشعياء | **0** |
| Jeremiah | ارميا | 142 | إرميا | **0** |
| Absalom | ابشالوم | 111 | أبشالوم | **0** |
| Jezebel | ايزابل | 23 | إيزابل | **0** |
| Job | ايوب | 59 | أيوب | **0** |
| Esther | استير | 55 | أستير | **0** |
| Gospel | الانجيل | 37 | الإنجيل | **0** |

Hamza is *not* absent from the text (ئ 10,744 · ء 6,797 · أ 7,430) — it is specifically the
word-initial hamza on these names that is written bare. A heading spelling أورشليم would look
different from every verse under it.

## Diacritics

The text carries almost no tashkeel — 7,607 marks in 2.36 M characters, and 7,599 of those are
SHADDA. So headings are written unvocalised, except where a name's own spelling carries a shadda
(نبوخذناصّر, منسّى), which is part of the name as this edition prints it.

Six languages, six forms for "the LORD": hr GOSPOD · de HERR · es Señor · fr l'Éternel ·
pt Senhor · ar الرب.
